03 / From inspiration to a decision

Fewer guesses.
Better comparisons.

Use this Kakobuy shopping guide to turn a long list of possibilities into a practical wardrobe shortlist. Keep comparable information together, and mark missing details instead of filling them in with assumptions.

Browse Kakobuy finds inspiration on CAFANS ↗

  1. Choose a category and purpose

    Decide whether you need daily sneakers, a warm layer, training clothing or a finishing accessory. Give each item a specific role before comparing alternatives.

  2. Save the exact listing and variant

    Record the source URL, seller, selected color, size and date checked. Two similar photographs can lead to different materials or product variants.

  3. Compare measurements, not labels

    Use a well-fitting item you own as a reference. For clothing, compare chest width, shoulder width and length; for trousers, compare waist, rise and inseam.

  4. Review the full purchase conditions

    Check stock, return terms, shipping availability and the complete cost. If a shopping service is involved, confirm its current restrictions and fees before paying.

04 / Your comparison notes

What belongs in your sheet?

A Kakobuy clothing spreadsheet becomes more useful when each row answers the same questions.

CategoryRecord these detailsResolve before ordering
Sneakers & shoes Model, color, size system, available foot-length guidance and sole details. Does the seller’s size chart match your measurements?
Hoodies, sweaters & T-shirts Chest width, shoulder width, body length, sleeve length and stated fabric. Are the measurements for the garment or for the wearer?
Jackets & sets Lining, closures, layering room and separate top and bottom measurements. Are all pictured pieces included in the selected option?
Pants & shorts Waist, rise, inseam, leg opening and stretch information. Is the waist measured relaxed, stretched or around the body?
Headwear & jerseys Hat circumference and adjustment; jersey chest width, length and fabric. Are customization, sizing and return conditions clear?
Underwear & accessories Size, composition, dimensions, fastening and care instructions. Do hygiene exclusions or material sensitivities affect the choice?

07 / Clear answers

Before you browse.

What is a Kakobuy spreadsheet?

A Kakobuy spreadsheet is a product research list used to organize shopping links, categories and comparison notes. Treat it as a starting point: verify the current listing, measurements, seller information and total cost before ordering.

Is this an official Kakobuy website?

No. This is an independent shopping guide with links to CAFANS. It does not operate Kakobuy or represent the brands shown.

Can I download a spreadsheet here?

This page provides a browsing directory and comparison guide, not a downloadable spreadsheet. Use the category links to explore CAFANS and record your own shortlist.

Does a listed price include international shipping?

Do not assume it does. Check whether the displayed price includes domestic delivery, service fees, international shipping and any applicable import charges.

Do the brand photographs show available products?

No. The photographs are editorial style references. They do not establish current availability, seller authorization or product authenticity on CAFANS.
